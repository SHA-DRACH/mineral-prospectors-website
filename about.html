Who we are

Technical expertise, grounded in local knowledge

Mineral Prospectors Sierra Leone is committed to excellence in mineral exploration, innovation, and responsible resource development. Our team combines technical expertise with local knowledge to support mining and investment opportunities across Sierra Leone.

We work alongside investors, mining companies and communities to make sure exploration is carried out accurately, safely and with respect for the environment.

Our mission

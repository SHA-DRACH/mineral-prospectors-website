Our Services

Professional geological and advisory services for exploration projects of every size.

01

Mineral Prospecting

Identification and evaluation of mineral deposits using modern exploration techniques.

We plan and carry out prospecting programmes that locate and evaluate mineral occurrences, combining desktop studies, reconnaissance and systematic follow-up so that promising ground is identified early and cost-effectively.

Enquire about this service
02

Geological Mapping

Field investigations and geological data collection for exploration projects.

Our field teams map lithology, structure and alteration, and record observations in a consistent digital format, giving your project a dependable geological foundation.

Enquire about this service
03

Sampling and Analysis

Soil, rock, and sediment sampling with laboratory support.

We design and run soil, rock chip and stream sediment sampling campaigns with proper quality control, and coordinate laboratory analysis and reporting of the results.

Enquire about this service
04

Mineral Resource Evaluation

Assessment of mineral potential for investors and mining companies.

Independent, clearly reported assessments of mineral potential that help investors and mining companies decide where to commit further exploration spending.

Enquire about this service
05

Mining Consultancy

Professional advice for exploration, licensing, and project planning.

Practical guidance on exploration strategy, licensing requirements and project planning in Sierra Leone, from first enquiry to a structured work programme.

Enquire about this service
06

Environmental Support

Guidance on responsible and sustainable mining practices.

Advice on responsible, sustainable practice so that exploration and mining activities respect the environment and the communities around them.

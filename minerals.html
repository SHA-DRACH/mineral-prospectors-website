Minerals We Work With

The commodities we prospect for, evaluate and advise on across Sierra Leone.

Gold

Hosted mainly in the greenstone belts of the north and east, alongside widespread alluvial occurrences.

Diamonds

Sierra Leone is renowned for its alluvial and kimberlite diamonds, particularly in the eastern districts.

Iron Ore

Large iron ore deposits occur in the northern part of the country.

Bauxite

The principal ore of aluminium, found in lateritic deposits in the south and west.

Rutile

A high-grade titanium mineral for which Sierra Leone’s coastal deposits are internationally recognised.

Ilmenite

A titanium-iron oxide mineral commonly found together with rutile in heavy mineral sands.

Other Valuable Minerals

Including zircon, platinum-group and other minerals identified through systematic exploration.
